# Tek Tek Oy — brand proposal (black and white)

Written for: the founders. A first direction to react to, not a final identity.

## Idea

"Tek, tek": two steps, one after the other. The logo shows that as two squares, one solid and one outlined, placed one step apart. Solid is what is done. Outline is what is next.

## Logo options

- **A — Tick-tock** (`logo-a-tick-tock.svg`): the two squares beside the wordmark on one line. Works for headers, email signatures and the website.
- **B — Stacked** (`logo-b-stacked.svg`): TEK / TEK / OY stacked in heavy caps. Works as a square sign, a sticker or a social avatar.

Before use, convert the text to outlines in Illustrator or Figma, so it looks the same on every machine.

## Color

- Black `#000000`: logo, text, main surfaces
- White `#FFFFFF`: background, reversed logo on black
- Grey `#AAAAAA`: secondary text only

No other colors for now.

## Typography

- Logo and headings: Space Grotesk Bold, uppercase, tight spacing
- Body: Inter Regular and Medium
- Both are free on Google Fonts.

## Voice

Short sentences. Plain words. Say what the company does, not how great it is.

## Clear space and size

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of one capital letter. Minimum width: 120 px on screen, 30 mm in print.

## Do not

- Stretch, rotate or outline the logo without permission
- Add gradients, shadows or colors
- Put the logo on a busy photo
